Ground truth is your Present/Absent review of the input image, saved per model and region. The location marker alone does not label presence. Present/Absent predictions below the run’s confidence threshold and uncertain answers abstain. Errors, abstentions and missing/unsure reviews are excluded from rates. A dash means no eligible denominator. These measure presence in each input: a crop for grid/BSP or the full image for direct bounding boxes. BSP parent and overlapping crops count separately. They do not measure exact localization, verified identity or scene-level search success.
